# Company Overview

VORTEX SECURE is a simulated international cybersecurity consulting and technology organization specializing in enterprise security, AI security, Purple Team operations, threat detection, penetration testing, security consulting, and security assessments.

The organization provides cybersecurity services to customers operating in sectors such as:

- Financial Services
- Healthcare
- Artificial Intelligence
- Technology
- Startups
- Enterprise Organizations

VORTEX SECURE helps organizations identify cybersecurity risks, improve security controls, strengthen security maturity, detect threats, respond to incidents, and maintain appropriate compliance and governance.

In addition to traditional cybersecurity services, VORTEX SECURE maintains a strong focus on Artificial Intelligence Security.

The organization assesses AI systems throughout their lifecycle, including:

- Foundation Models
- Fine-Tuned Models
- AI Agents
- RAG Systems
- Training Datasets
- Model Weights
- AI APIs
- AI Development Infrastructure

AI security activities include model security assessments, adversarial testing, AI red teaming, prompt-injection testing, model integrity validation, AI monitoring, and security assessments of AI-enabled applications and agents.

VORTEX SECURE is a simulated international cybersecurity organization operating a complex hybrid enterprise environment with approximately 500 employees and offices across the United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, and United States.
